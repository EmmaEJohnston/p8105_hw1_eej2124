# should i include mean of “numeric vector”/“random sample”?

The mean of the logical vector is 0. The mean of the character vector is
NA. The mean of the factor vector is NA. You cannot take the mean of
neither the character nor factor vectors.

# Write as.numeric code

``` r
as_numeric_logical = 
  as.numeric(pull(df, vec_logical))

as_numeric_logical

as_numeric_character = 
  as.numeric(pull(df, vec_character))

as_numeric_character

as_numeric_factor = 
  as.numeric(pull(df, vec_factor))

as_numeric_factor
```

When you convert the logical variable to numeric, you get an output of
1s and 0s. However, when you convert the character and factor vectors to
numeric, you get an output of ‘NA’. This shows why you cannot take the
mean of these variables, R cannot understand them as numeric.
